**HeroPhotoBand** — full-width band with full-bleed automotive photography and an UPPERCASE display-xl headline. The photo is the band; a left-to-right scrim keeps type legible. Pass a `<Button variant="outline">` as children.

```jsx
<HeroPhotoBand title="More BMW M." subtitle="The most powerful letter in the world." image="/hero.jpg">
  <Button variant="outline">Discover the models</Button>
</HeroPhotoBand>
```
