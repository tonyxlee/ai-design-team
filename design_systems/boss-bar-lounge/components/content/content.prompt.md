Boss Bar's content building blocks, all sharp-cornered on dark surfaces with gold-glow hover.

- **Badge** — tag chips, the "Popular" flag, and rounded language pills.
- **PriceCard** — a choice-system price tier; `highlight` for the featured one.
- **GlowImage** — gallery/staff image that lifts with a gold glow + slow zoom.
- **MenuItem** — one drinks-menu row (gold dot, name, price + "k" unit).
- **NumberedReason** — the "5 Reasons" list row with a glowing serif numeral.
- **StepCard** — a how-to step with a big ghost numeral and connector arrow.
- **NewsCard** — article card with 16:9 thumb, serif title, clamped excerpt.
- **FaqItem** — self-managed accordion row.

```jsx
<PriceCard count="3" fee="900,000 ₫" highlight badge="Popular" />
<MenuItem name="Macallan 12y" price="6,800" premium />
<NumberedReason number="01" title="Da Nang's Largest Venue" tag="300㎡">A refined interior…</NumberedReason>
```
