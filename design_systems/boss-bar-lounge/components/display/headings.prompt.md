Boss Bar's section header system — pair an `Eyebrow` kicker with a serif display title; the trailing emphasis renders as the signature italic-gold treatment.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Why Boss Bar?"
  title="5 Reasons to Choose"
  emphasis="Boss Bar Lounge"
  lead="Among the many bars in Da Nang, here is why guests choose Boss Bar."
/>
```

`Eyebrow` can be used on its own. Both accept `color`/`eyebrowColor` of `tan` (default) or `gold` — pricing and how-to sections use gold. Use `align="center"` for CTA-style centered headers.
